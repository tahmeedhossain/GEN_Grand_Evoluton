## 2. Abstraction edges

A route (Part I) says that a target follows from its inputs. An abstraction edge relates the extensions of two nodes. Kinds:

- member, excluded: every state (datum) denoted by the source is in the cluster, or none is.
- cut: only part of the source is in the cluster. Cuts are recorded and not used in the closure.
- subset, equal, complement, disjoint: between clusters.
- aggregate: the target is a sum, a direct sum or a moment of its sources.
- criterion: a model-layer node supplies the predicate of the cluster. test: an observable-layer functional decides membership. lineage: a model was absorbed.
- hosts, reify, refines: a model hosts a cluster; an observable-layer cluster is taken as an object of the state layer of a resource theory; an inclusion of process classes or of models (Part I cross-edge kept as recorded).

Criterion types. Four are the typology of free-operation justifications of the resource-theory network (node F-TYPE, the family attribute of each theory, proposition QP1): invariance under a group (sym), membership in a subtheory (sub), factorisation over a partition (par), a constraint (con). The released data use five more: deficit against a reference model with a witness (def), same process type or system (fam), aggregation (agg), substitution in a route (str), model lineage (lin). Counts over stated criterion, member, cut and aggregate edges: sym 36, fam 23, def 16, agg 12, str 12, lin 11, par 10, sub 9, con 7, none 2.

Well-formedness (three legs, one per layer). A cluster node is well formed when it has a criterion edge from the model layer, a test edge from the observable layer and at least one member. For an effect the criterion is a no-go edge from a model or an axiom.

Total: 446 abstraction edges, 293 stated and 153 derived by closure. Origin of the stated ones: 204 taken from the released graphs (edges, partitions, clusters, families) and from the cross-edges and glue concepts of Part I, 39 from the theory band of the resource-theory network (model, free set, resource set of one theory), 22 read from released statements (SS2, SS3b, SS4, SS4b, SS5, SS5b, SS6, SS9, SS19), 28 curated on 18 November 2026 (13 assign a sector of the Standard Model graph to its Wigner class; 15 are memberships or cuts of system-specific nodes in generic clusters). 79 of the stated edges restate a released route.

## 3. Operations and rules

Operations added: CLUSTER (f ; U → c), TEST (w → c), AGGREGATE (U → c), LIFT, DESCEND, REFINE, REIFY (an observable-layer cluster placed in the state layer of a resource theory, a glue with a role flip).

Rules added (R1 to R16 are in Part II).

- R17, state clustering. Guard: a model-layer node f supplies a predicate that separates state nodes, or routes into one target differ only by one leg. Action: add the cluster node, its complement where a carrier is fixed, the criterion edge from f and the member edges. R1 (symmetry partition, GEN_HEP) is the case of an invariance inside one system.
- R18, observable clustering, three forms. Family: observables of one process type are members of a family node. Aggregate: a node is a sum or a moment of its members. Effect: given a reference model m0 and a functional w that m0 bounds, the effect is the set of observable data outside the bound, with a no-go edge from m0, the test edge from w and the member edges.
- R19, lift. Guard: two clusters with members and no edge between them, and either a member-level route from one into the other or an implication of the incidence table. Action: add the edge with status open; rule R14 closes it.
- R20, descent. Guard: an inclusion a ⊆ b with status theorem or definition, a member u of a, u an object of the kind on which b is defined, and the frame of the inclusion holds for u. Action: add u ∈ b with status derived, and the excluded edge for the complement. With a witness of b and measured data of u the status is observed; no membership in the released data has that status, so R20 fires here only through the closure.
- R21, refinement. Guard: a derived fact contradicts a stated one, or an implication of the incidence table is refuted by an object. Action: split the cluster, add the counterexample as an atom, or restrict the inclusion to a frame.

Relation to the history graphs. In GEN_HEP an observable with no route from the current model (rule R2) drives a change of the model and is resolved within a few steps. An effect of the generic tier is the same deficit with the reference model kept fixed: the set of data that the reference model cannot produce becomes a node, and the witness is its test. In the released data 6 of the 13 effects carry such a no-go edge from a model or axiom node; the other 7 are flagged (P6).

## 4. The combined pipeline

A stage is a phase in a tier. Phases: the eight of Part II (observe, deficit, hypothesize, restructure, predict, falsify, calibrate, reduce) followed by cluster and descend. Tier of an event: the tier of the node, of the target of the route, or of the two ends of the abstraction edge; an abstraction edge with one end in each tier counts as crossing (row "across"). The attribution is a lookup table (`two_tier/pipeline.py`): a node of a cluster sublayer, or a route that an abstraction edge restates, moves to the phase cluster; an edge derived by closure is in the phase descend.

## 6. Closure of the memberships (rule R20)

Facts are signed pairs (u, c). u ∈ c means that every state denoted by the node u lies in c; u ∉ c means that none does. Rules:

1. u ∈ a and a ⊆ b give u ∈ b.
2. u ∉ b and a ⊆ b give u ∉ a.
3. u ∈ a and a, b disjoint give u ∉ b.
4. u ∉ a and b the complement of a give u ∈ b.

Rules 1 to 3 hold for arbitrary sets. Rule 4 needs u inside the carrier of the pair, so a conclusion about c is drawn only when u is an object of the kind on which c is defined (domain tags: any, bipartite, qudit, continuous variable, behaviour, device). An inclusion stated under a frame is used only for atoms that carry the frame: E, the coherence basis is the eigenbasis of a nondegenerate Hamiltonian; P, the atom is bipartite with a local product coherence basis. The fixed point is the least one; each derived fact keeps the edges it comes from. The tags were assigned by hand (`two_tier/abstraction.py`, tables INCLUSIONS, ATOM_DOMAINS, ATOM_FRAMES).

Result: 40 stated facts on 17 atoms, 15 inclusions, 2 disjointness edges, the complement pairs and the glue give 153 derived facts at node level (57 memberships, 96 exclusions) and 0 conflicts. 53 of them concern tier-C atoms. Derived memberships of four of them: blackbody radiation: Gibbs state gamma (unique), Symmetric (G-invariant) states, TTS / energy-diagonal states, Wigner-positive states; particle in a box: Incoherent (diagonal) states, Real states, Symmetric (G-invariant) states; quantum harmonic oscillator: Incoherent (diagonal) states, Real states, Symmetric (G-invariant) states; a wave packet: Asymmetric states (clocks/frames), Athermal states.

Incidence table of 18 atoms and the 28 state classes that remain after glue: 274 applicable cells, 129 settled (40 stated, 81 derived only, 8 cuts). The other 72 derived facts repeat a settled cell on the second node of a glue class. The 145 empty cells are open items in the sense of R13.

Refinements made while building (rule R21). A first closure run derived that the Gibbs state is separable through QRT:SS1 and that a single-particle stationary state is "not entangled". Both are wrong readings: the energy eigenbasis of an interacting Hamiltonian is not a product basis (QRT:SS3 records the Gibbs state of a generic interacting H as magic), and entanglement is not defined for a single system. The inclusions were restricted to frames and every conclusion to the domain of its cluster.

## 7. Kernel: ten free sets on explicit two-qubit states

Purpose: check that the inclusion structure of the generic tier is recoverable from concrete members. Objects: 13 two-qubit density matrices, of which 8 realise 7 of the 9 distinguished states of the resource-theory network (the bound entangled state and the Wigner-positive magic state have no two-qubit realisation) and 5 are reference states; 2 more were added in a second round. Frame: H = (ωA/2) Z⊗1 + (ωB/2) 1⊗Z, ωA = 1, ωB = 1.5, β = 2 (nondegenerate total spectrum). Attributes: membership in the free sets of purity, athermality, asymmetry, coherence, imaginarity, magic (linear program over the 60 two-qubit stabilizer states), discord (numerical minimisation over local bases), entanglement (PPT, exact for two qubits), steering and Bell nonlocality (CHSH by the Horodecki criterion; separable ⇒ unsteerable ⇒ local). Four cells are imported thresholds for the Werner states at p = 0.4 and p = 0.6.

## 8. Proposals for the released graphs (not applied)

- P1 (GEN_QRT_Level; missing literature edge). Add the inclusion sF-dis ⊂ sF-ent (zero-discord states are separable). The nonclassicality network has it as edge S7; the resource-theory network has no edge at sF-dis. Evidence: Kernel: dis ⇒ ent holds on all objects and is not derivable from the released QRT edges alone.
- P2 (GEN_QRT_Level; elementary edge). Add the inclusion sF-coh ⊂ sF-dis for a local product incoherent basis: a state diagonal in a product basis is classical-classical by the definition of GPT:s-zerodisc. Edge SS1 is then the composition of this edge with P1. Evidence: Kernel: coh ⇒ dis holds on all objects; derivable only through GPT:R1, a proposition derived in the project.
- P3 (GEN_QRT_Level; statement). Edge SS2 lists the free sets that contain I/d; the classical-classical set is missing from the list. Evidence: Kernel: pur ⇒ dis; I/4 is diagonal in every product basis.
- P4 (GEN_QRT_Level; missing atom). No distinguished state is a stabilizer state with an imaginary density matrix. With the states the network names, STAB ⊂ real cannot be refuted; |+i> refutes it. Evidence: Kernel round 0: mag ⇒ ima is a candidate; round 1: refuted by |+i>|0>.
- P5 (GEN_QRT_Level, GEN_GPT_Level; nodes without an edge). 25 of the 116 nodes of the resource-theory network have no edge; each belongs to its theory only through the node attribute "theory": model m-ste; free sets sF-dis, sF-inc, sF-nga; free operations pF-dis, pF-ima, pF-mag, pF-nga; use protocols pU-dis, pU-inc, pU-nga, pU-pur; detectors wD-asy, wD-ath, wD-inc, wD-nga, wD-pur; quantifiers wQ-coh, wQ-ctx, wQ-dis, wQ-ima, wQ-mag, wQ-nga, wQ-pur, wQ-ste. In the nonclassicality network 2 of 92 nodes have no edge (p-incompset, p-pauli). For the purity theory this leaves the free set and the resource set without a test edge. Evidence: Count of released edges per node (86 edges in QRT v1.2, 156 in GPT v2.6). Three-leg lint: classes without a test are s-bound, s-gauss, sF-pur, sR-pur.
- P6 (GEN_GPT_Level; missing reference models). 6 of 13 effects have a no-go edge from the model layer. The other effects are defined in their node text as the absence of a model of a named kind (local hidden states, noncontextual value assignments, a joint distribution), but no node stands for that model. Evidence: Three-leg lint of the effects, Checks view.
- P7 (GEN_GPT_Level; missing criterion). The clusters s-gauss and s-bound have no criterion edge and no test edge. For s-gauss the model node m-gaussian exists and no edge joins the two. Evidence: Three-leg lint of the state clusters.
- P8 (GEN_QRT_Level; scope). Edge SS12 states that the Gibbs state is incoherent in the energy basis for a nondegenerate Hamiltonian. The Gibbs state is a function of H and is therefore diagonal in every eigenbasis of H, degenerate or not. The closure here keeps the released scope. Evidence: Elementary; not used in the closure.
- P9 (all; cross-tier edges). 18 stated membership and cut edges run from system-specific nodes into generic clusters. They are candidates for cross-network edges of the released graphs. Evidence: Clusters view, filter "With a member from the other tier"; Checks view, direction of the membership edges.
- P10 (GEN_QRT_Level, GEN_GPT_Level; derived memberships). The closure derives 153 facts (57 memberships, 96 exclusions) from 40 stated ones, with 0 conflicts. Each is a candidate edge with its derivation. On the 28 classes that remain after glue they settle 81 cells of the incidence table that had no stated fact. Evidence: Kernel cross-check: 125 comparisons of 112 facts with the computed table; 125 agree.

## 9. Limits

- Years are the ingredient chronology of Part II, not historical dates. Curated edges are dated 2026.
- The 28 curated edges rest on the node definitions as written in the released data. Those with status standard are textbook identifications that were not derived here.
- The frame and domain tags of the closure were assigned by hand.
- Kernel: tolerance 1e-9; the discord test is a numerical minimisation; the cell "Bell-local" of the Werner state at p = 0.6 holds for projective measurements only (Acín, Gisin, Toner, arXiv:quant-ph/0606138); locality under general measurements is not established at this p.
- Only the single-premise implications of the incidence table were computed, not a full attribute exploration.
